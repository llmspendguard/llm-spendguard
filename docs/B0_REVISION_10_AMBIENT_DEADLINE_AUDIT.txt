Scope and limitations
  Mandatory teardown/accounting locks remain untimed: connection live/pending
  count publication/rollback, lease decrement/notification, cursor registration
  release, and shutdown cleanup must complete to preserve resource ownership.
  They are not new capacity admission or deliberate contention retries.
  ledger_writer.py:516 close_ledger_writers is referenced by tests and atexit;
  worker idle-queue waits and expiry-sweeper cadence are background lifecycle
  waits, not request admission. Maintenance-only raw SQLite opens do not acquire
  pool capacity. Filesystem I/O, OS scheduling and mandatory teardown do not have
  hard realtime guarantees; this audit closes admission deadline propagation,
  not arbitrary blocking kernel I/O or process scheduling.
